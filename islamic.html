01. The Call to Prayer (Al-Adhaan) 14 Q

💡 The Mu'adh-dhin is the person who calls the adhaan five times a day at the Masjid.
Wording of the Adhaan:
1. Allaahu Akbar (×4) — Allah is The Most Great
2. Ash-hadu allaa-ilaaha illallaah (×2) — I testify there is no God but Allah
3. Ash-hadu anna Muhammadar Rasoolullaah (×2) — I testify Muhammad ﷺ is the Messenger of Allah
4. Hayya 'alas salaah (×2) — Hasten to the prayer
5. Hayya 'alal falaah (×2) — Hasten to success
6. Allaahu Akbar (×2)
7. Laa-ilaaha illallaah — There is no God but Allah

In Fajr adhaan: As-Salaatu khayrum-minan nawm — The prayer is better than sleep

📖 Think Questions

1. What should we reply when the mu'adh-dhin says "Hayya 'alas salaah"?
We should say: "Laa hawla wa laa quwwata illaa billaah" — There is no power and no might except with Allah.
2. Why is it different from other replies of the adhaan?
Because "Hayya 'alas salaah" and "Hayya 'alal falaah" are a call to come to prayer. We cannot call others, so instead we say that we have no power to obey except with Allah's help.

02. Al-Iqaamah 12 Q

💡 Al-Iqaamah is the call to the START of fardh salah. It tells us the prayer is now beginning.
Wording of the Iqaamah:
1. Allaahu Akbar, Allaahu Akbar
2. Ash-hadu allaa-ilaaha illallaah
3. Ash-hadu anna Muhammadar Rasoolullaah
4. Hayya 'alas salaah, Hayya 'alal falaah
5. Qad qaamatis-salaah (×2) — The prayer is now ready
6. Allaahu Akbar, Allaahu Akbar
7. Laa-ilaaha illallaah

🔗 Match the Wording to its Meaning

Tap a meaning on the left, then tap its Arabic on the right.

📖 Written Questions

1. What is the Iqaamah?
The Iqaamah is the message that the prayer is starting. It tells everyone that the prayer is beginning and they should join the jamaa'ah and face the qiblah.
2. How is it different from the adhaan?
The adhaan is called to announce the TIME of salah has come. The Iqaamah is called to announce the salah is STARTING right now.
3. Write three points why salah is success.
1. Salah brings us closer to Allah and earns His blessings.
2. Salah keeps us away from sins and bad deeds.
3. Salah gives us peace, discipline and blessings in life.

03. Adam (عليه السلام) 20 Q

💡 Allah made the first man and called him Adam. He was also the first Prophet.

04. Prophet ﷺ — Supporter of the Poor 12 Q

💡 During a famine, the Prophet ﷺ took Ali (رضي الله عنه) into his care and Al-Abbaas took Ja'far, to help their uncle Abu Taalib.

📖 Etiquettes of Helping Others

What are the 4 manners of helping others we learnt?
1. Help in secret for Allah's sake alone.
2. Prefer your relatives first.
3. Identify the needy, for they are shy to ask.
4. Don't remind people of your favours.

06. Lying, Gossiping & Backbiting 18 Q

💡 The tongue is a great blessing from Allah. Even though it is small, it is difficult to control.
Remember the three meanings:
• Lying — to say things that are not true
• Gossiping — to talk about other people's private lives which might be unkind or not true
• Backbiting — to talk about your brother or sister who is not there, in a manner they do not like, EVEN IF IT IS TRUE

07. Surah Al-Faatihah 16 Q

💡 Al-Faatihah is the FIRST surah of the Qur'an. It is a Makkan surah with SEVEN aayaat (verses).
Translation of the Surah:
1. In the Name of Allah, the Most Kind, the Most Merciful.
2. All praise is due to Allah, the Lord of all the worlds;
3. The Most Kind, the Most Merciful;
4. Master of the Day of Judgement.
5. You alone we worship and to You alone we turn for help.
6. Guide us to the Straight Path.
7. The path of those on whom You have bestowed Your Grace; not of those who earned Your Anger, nor of those who have gone astray.

08. Pure Water 16 Q

💡 Water is a great gift from Allah. From water, Allah made every living creature.
Purity of water is seen in 3 ways:
1. Colour — should be clear
2. Smell — should have no smell at all
3. Taste — should not be completely changed
