🔤 Section A: Vowels & Consonants (ಸ್ವರಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ವ್ಯಂಜನಗಳು)

ಕನ್ನಡ: ಸರಿಯಾದ ಉತ್ತರವನ್ನು ಆಯ್ಕೆಮಾಡಿ.
English: Choose the correct answer.

1. ಕನ್ನಡದ ಮೊದಲ ಸ್ವರ ಯಾವುದು? / Which is the FIRST vowel in Kannada?

💡 ಸ್ವರ = vowel. The first one starts with the 'a' sound.
✅ ಅ is the first vowel in Kannada. The vowels are: ಅ, ಆ, ಇ, ಈ, ಉ, ಊ, ಋ, ಎ, ಏ, ಐ, ಒ, ಓ, ಔ, ಅಂ, ಅಃ.

2. ಕನ್ನಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಷ್ಟು ಸ್ವರಗಳಿವೆ? / How many vowels are there in Kannada?

💡 Count: ಅ, ಆ, ಇ, ಈ, ಉ, ಊ, ಋ, ಎ, ಏ, ಐ, ಒ, ಓ, ಔ, ಅಂ, ಅಃ
✅ There are 15 vowels in Kannada (ಅ to ಅಃ) - counting the two special ones ಅಂ (anusvara) and ಅಃ (visarga).

3. ಇವುಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಯಾವುದು ಸ್ವರ? / Which one is a vowel?

💡 Vowels can be spoken alone without any other letter.
✅ ಇ (i) is a vowel. The others (ಕ, ಮ, ತ) are consonants (ವ್ಯಂಜನ).

4. ಕನ್ನಡದ ಮೊದಲ ವ್ಯಂಜನ ಯಾವುದು? / Which is the FIRST consonant in Kannada?

💡 ವ್ಯಂಜನ = consonant. The first consonant sound is 'ka'.
✅ ಕ (ka) is the first consonant in Kannada. Consonants start after vowels end.

5. ಅ ಇಂದ ನಂತರ ಬರುವ ಸ್ವರ ಯಾವುದು? / Which vowel comes AFTER ಅ?

💡 ಅ is short 'a' and the next one is long 'aa'.
✅ After ಅ (a) comes ಆ (aa). The order is: ಅ → ಆ → ಇ → ಈ → ...

6. ಇ, ಈ, ___, ಊ — ಕಾಣೆಯಾದ ಸ್ವರ ಯಾವುದು? / Which vowel is missing in: ಇ, ಈ, ___, ಊ?

💡 The sound 'u' comes between 'ee' and 'oo'.
✅ The vowel order is ಇ (i), ಈ (ee), ಉ (u), ಊ (oo). So ಉ comes between ಈ and ಊ.

7. ಕನ್ನಡದ ಕೊನೆಯ ಸ್ವರ ಯಾವುದು? / Which is the LAST vowel in Kannada?

💡 It is the one with two dots after ಅ.
✅ ಅಃ (visarga) is the last vowel. The last two are special: ಅಂ (anusvara) and ಅಃ (visarga).

8. ಇವುಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಯಾವುದು ಸ್ವರ ಅಲ್ಲ? / Which one is NOT a vowel?

💡 Three of these are vowels and one is a consonant.
✅ ಗ (ga) is a consonant, not a vowel. ಅ, ಇ, ಉ are all vowels.

🔢 Section B: Kannada Numbers (ಕನ್ನಡ ಅಂಕಿಗಳು)

ಕನ್ನಡ: ಕನ್ನಡ ಅಂಕಿಯ ಇಂಗ್ಲೀಷ್ ಹೆಸರು ಬರೆಯಿರಿ.
English: Write the English name of the Kannada number.

1. ೧ ಇದರ ಹೆಸರು? / What is ೧ called?

💡 It looks like a Kannada letter but it is the first counting number.
✅ ೧ = 1 = One = ಒಂದು (ondu).

2. ೫ ಇದರ ಹೆಸರು? / What is ೫ called in Kannada?

💡 5 in Kannada starts with the 'ai' sound.
✅ ೫ = 5 = ಐದು (aidu) = Five.

3. ಹತ್ತು (Ten) ಇದನ್ನು ಕನ್ನಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೇಗೆ ಬರೆಯುತ್ತೇವೆ? / How do we write Ten (10) in Kannada?

💡 It is written with two Kannada digits: ೧ and ೦.
✅ ೧೦ = 10 = ಹತ್ತು (hattu) = Ten.

4. ೧, ೨, ___, ೪, ೫ — ಕಾಣೆಯಾದ ಅಂಕಿ ಯಾವುದು? / Which number is missing in: ೧, ೨, ___, ೪, ೫?

💡 What comes between 2 and 4?
✅ ೩ = 3 = ಮೂರು (mooru) comes between ೨ and ೪.

5. ೭ = ? / What is ೭?

💡 The number after 6 (ಆರು).
✅ ೭ = 7 = ಏಳು (eelu) = Seven.

6. ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತು (Twenty) ಇದನ್ನು ಕನ್ನಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೇಗೆ ಬರೆಯುತ್ತೇವೆ? / How do we write Twenty (20) in Kannada?

💡 2 followed by 0 in Kannada digits.
✅ ೨೦ = 20 = ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತು (ippattu) = Twenty.

7. ಹದಿನೈದು (Fifteen) ಇದನ್ನು ಕನ್ನಡ ಅಂಕಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬರೆಯಿರಿ. / Write Fifteen in Kannada numerals.

💡 It is 10 + 5 in Kannada digits.
✅ ೧೫ = 15 = ಹದಿನೈದು (hadinaidu) = Fifteen.

🖼️ Section C: Match Picture with Word (ಚಿತ್ರ ಹೊಂದಿಸಿ)

ಕನ್ನಡ: ಚಿತ್ರವನ್ನು ಸರಿಯಾದ ಪದದೊಂದಿಗೆ ಹೊಂದಿಸಿ.
English: Match the picture with the correct word.

1. 🥭 ಈ ಹಣ್ಣಿನ ಕನ್ನಡ ಹೆಸರು? / What is Mango called in Kannada?

💡 Mango sounds like 'maavu' in Kannada.
✅ Mango = ಮಾವಿನ ಹಣ್ಣು (maavina hannu). 'ಹಣ್ಣು' means fruit.

2. 🍎 ಈ ಹಣ್ಣಿನ ಕನ್ನಡ ಹೆಸರು? / What is Apple called in Kannada?

💡 Short word that sounds like 'sebu'.
✅ Apple = ಸೇಬು (sebu).

3. 🍌 ಈ ಹಣ್ಣಿನ ಕನ್ನಡ ಹೆಸರು? / What is Banana called in Kannada?

💡 Starts with 'baale...' and ends with 'hannu' (fruit).
✅ Banana = ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು (baalehannu). 'ಬಾಳೆ' means banana tree.

4. ಪಪ್ಪಾಯಿ ಹಣ್ಣಿನ ಇಂಗ್ಲೀಷ್ ಹೆಸರು? / What is ಪಪ್ಪಾಯಿ called in English?

💡 Sounds very similar in both languages.
✅ ಪಪ್ಪಾಯಿ (pappayi) = Papaya. The Kannada word sounds similar to the English word.

5. ಗಜ್ಜರಿ = ? / What does ಗಜ್ಜರಿ mean in English?

💡 The orange vegetable that rabbits love!
✅ ಗಜ್ಜರಿ (gajjari) = Carrot. It is an orange vegetable.

6. ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ = ? / What does ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ mean in English?

💡 This vegetable makes your eyes water when you cut it.
✅ ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ (eerulli) = Onion. The vegetable that makes you cry!

7. ಟೊಮೆಟೊ = ? / What does ಟೊಮೆಟೊ mean in English?

💡 The red round vegetable - sounds the same in English!
✅ ಟೊಮೆಟೊ (tomato) = Tomato. Sounds very similar in both languages.

8. ಆಲೂಗಡ್ಡೆ = ? / What does ಆಲೂಗಡ್ಡೆ mean in English?

💡 Used to make chips and fries.
✅ ಆಲೂಗಡ್ಡೆ (aalugadde) = Potato. The brown root vegetable used in chips!

9. ಕಲ್ಲಂಗಡಿ = ? / What does ಕಲ್ಲಂಗಡಿ mean in English?

💡 Big green fruit with red juicy inside.
✅ ಕಲ್ಲಂಗಡಿ (kallangadi) = Watermelon. The big green fruit with red inside.

10. Radish ಇದರ ಕನ್ನಡ ಹೆಸರು? / What is Radish called in Kannada?

💡 White long vegetable, starts with 'moo...'
✅ Radish = ಮೂಲಂಗಿ (moolangi). It is a white root vegetable.

✏️ Section D: Fill in the Blanks (ಖಾಲಿ ಜಾಗ ತುಂಬಿ)

ಕನ್ನಡ: ಖಾಲಿ ಜಾಗವನ್ನು ಸರಿಯಾದ ಕಾಗುಣಿತದಿಂದ ತುಂಬಿ.
English: Fill in the blank with the correct compound letter (kaagunita).

1. ಕ್ + ಆ = ? / What is ಕ್ + ಆ?

💡 Consonant + ಆ = long 'aa' sound.
✅ ಕ್ + ಆ = ಕಾ (kaa). When we add a vowel to a consonant, we get a compound letter.

2. ಕ್ + ಇ = ? / What is ಕ್ + ಇ?

💡 ಇ makes the 'i' sound.
✅ ಕ್ + ಇ = ಕಿ (ki).

3. ಗ್ + ಆ = ? / What is ಗ್ + ಆ?

💡 Consonant 'ga' + long 'aa' sound.
✅ ಗ್ + ಆ = ಗಾ (gaa).

4. ಕ _ ಟೆ (Hint: Flower) / Fill the blank: ಕ _ ಟೆ

💡 The middle letter sounds like 'ma'.
✅ ಕಮಟೆ... Note: this is for practicing letter recognition. Try reading the whole word!

5. ಚ್ + ಇ = ? / What is ಚ್ + ಇ?

💡 The 'cha' sound + 'i' = 'chi'.
✅ ಚ್ + ಇ = ಚಿ (chi).

📖 Section E: Write English Meanings (ಅರ್ಥ ಬರೆಯಿರಿ)

ಕನ್ನಡ: ಕನ್ನಡ ಪದಗಳ ಇಂಗ್ಲೀಷ್ ಅರ್ಥ ಬರೆಯಿರಿ.
English: Write the English meaning of the Kannada words.

1. ಹಯ (ಕುದುರೆ) = ? / What is the English meaning of ಹಯ?

💡 The hint ಕುದುರೆ in brackets means horse.
✅ ಹಯ (haya) = ಕುದುರೆ (kudure) = Horse.

2. ಧನ = ? / What does ಧನ mean in English?

💡 What we use to buy things.
✅ ಧನ (dhana) = Money. It also means wealth.

3. ಭಟ = ? / What does ಭಟ mean in English?

💡 Someone who fights in a battle.
✅ ಭಟ (bhata) = Soldier or Warrior. A brave person who fights.

4. ಕದ = ? / What does ಕದ mean in English?

💡 We open this to enter a room.
✅ ಕದ (kada) = Door. We open and close it to enter a room.

5. ಚಕ್ರ = ? / What does ಚಕ್ರ mean in English?

💡 Round shape, found on cars and bikes.
✅ ಚಕ್ರ (chakra) = Wheel. A round thing that rolls, like on a car.

6. ಮಗ = ? / What does ಮಗ mean in English?

💡 A boy child of parents.
✅ ಮಗ (maga) = Son. A boy child.

7. ವಚನ = ? / What does ವಚನ mean in English?

💡 When we give our word to someone.
✅ ವಚನ (vachana) = Promise or Word. A promise we give to someone.

👶 Section F: Baby Letters / Ottakshara (ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ)

ಕನ್ನಡ: ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ ಗುರುತಿಸಿ.
English: Identify the baby letter (ottakshara).

1. ಅಕ್ಕ ಪದದಲ್ಲಿ ಯಾವ ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರವಿದೆ? / Which baby letter (ottakshara) is in the word ಅಕ್ಕ (akka = elder sister)?

💡 Say 'akka' slowly - you hear 'k' twice.
✅ ಅಕ್ಕ has the baby letter ಕ್ಕ (double ka sound). ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ means baby letter.

2. ಅಜ್ಜ (ajja = grandfather) ಪದದಲ್ಲಿ ಯಾವ ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರವಿದೆ? / Which ottakshara is in the word ಅಜ್ಜ?

💡 Say 'ajja' - double 'j' sound.
✅ ಅಜ್ಜ has the baby letter ಜ್ಜ (double ja).

3. ಬೆಟ್ಟ (betta = hill) ಪದದಲ್ಲಿ ಯಾವ ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರವಿದೆ? / Which ottakshara is in the word ಬೆಟ್ಟ?

💡 Say 'betta' - the 't' sound is doubled.
✅ ಬೆಟ್ಟ has the baby letter ಟ್ಟ (double ta). It means hill or mountain.

4. 👁 Eye ಇದರ ಕನ್ನಡ ಹೆಸರು? / What is Eye called in Kannada?

💡 The part of the face we see with.
✅ Eye = ಕಣ್ಣು (kannu). It has the baby letter ಣ್ಣ (double na sound).

5. ಕಬ್ಬು = ? / What does ಕಬ್ಬು mean in English?

💡 The long green plant used to make sugar.
✅ ಕಬ್ಬು (kabbu) = Sugarcane. The long green plant we get sugar from. It has the baby letter ಬ್ಬ.

6. ಅಮ್ಮ = ? / What does ಅಮ್ಮ mean in English?

💡 The person who takes care of us at home.
✅ ಅಮ್ಮ (amma) = Mother. It has the baby letter ಮ್ಮ (double ma).

7. ಬಸ್ಸು = ? / What does ಬಸ್ಸು mean in English?

💡 A big vehicle that goes to school with many children.
✅ ಬಸ್ಸು (bassu) = Bus. The big vehicle that carries many people.

8. ಬೆಕ್ಕು = ? / What does ಬೆಕ್ಕು mean in English?

💡 The animal that says 'meow'.
✅ ಬೆಕ್ಕು (bekku) = Cat. The small furry animal that says meow.

📝 Section G: Dictation Words (ಪದಗಳನ್ನು ಬರೆಯಿರಿ)

ಕನ್ನಡ: ಪದಗಳನ್ನು ಓದಿ ಅರ್ಥ ಬರೆಯಿರಿ.
English: Read the words and write their meanings.

1. ಕಾಗದ = ? / What does ಕಾಗದ mean in English?

💡 We write and draw on it.
✅ ಕಾಗದ (kaagada) = Paper. We write on it.

2. ಕಿವಿ = ? / What does ಕಿವಿ mean in English?

💡 The part of our body we use to listen.
✅ ಕಿವಿ (kivi) = Ear. The body part used to hear.

3. ಕುರಿ = ? / What does ಕುರಿ mean in English?

💡 The woolly white animal that gives us wool for sweaters.
✅ ಕುರಿ (kuri) = Sheep. The woolly animal that gives us wool.

4. ಕೋಳಿ = ? / What does ಕೋಳಿ mean in English?

💡 The farm bird that gives us eggs.
✅ ಕೋಳಿ (koli) = Hen or Chicken. The bird that gives us eggs.

5. ಊರು = ? / What does ಊರು mean in English?

💡 The place where we live - our hometown.
✅ ಊರು (ooru) = Village, city, or one's hometown.

6. ಗೃಹ = ? / What does ಗೃಹ mean in English?

💡 The building where our family lives.
✅ ಗೃಹ (gruha) = House or Home. The place where we live.

7. ಐರಾವತ = ? / What does ಐರಾವತ mean in English?

💡 The biggest land animal with a long trunk.
✅ ಐರಾವತ (airavata) = Elephant (a special name for the king of elephants in mythology).

8. ಬೀಗ = ? / What does ಬೀಗ mean in English?

💡 We use this to keep doors closed and safe.
✅ ಬೀಗ (beega) = Lock. We use it to keep doors closed and safe.

🦋 Section H: Chitte Poem (ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ ಪದ್ಯ)

ಕನ್ನಡ: ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ ಪದ್ಯದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಉತ್ತರಿಸಿ.
English: Answer about the Chitte (Butterfly) poem.

1. ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ ಪದ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ 'ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ' ಇದರ ಇಂಗ್ಲೀಷ್ ಅರ್ಥ? / What does 'ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ' mean in the poem?

💡 The colourful flying insect with wings.
✅ ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ (chitte) = Butterfly. The colourful flying insect in the poem.

2. ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ ಪದ್ಯವನ್ನು ಬರೆದವರು ಯಾರು? / Who wrote the Chitte poem?

💡 The poet's name is in the textbook, at the bottom of the poem page.
✅ Dr. P. Narayana Bhat (ಡಾ. ಪಿ. ನಾರಾಯಣ ಭಟ್) wrote the Chitte poem. He is from Peraji village in Kodagu district.

3. 'ತಟ್ಟನೆ' ಇದರ ಅರ್ಥ? / What does 'ತಟ್ಟನೆ' mean?

💡 When something happens very fast.
✅ ತಟ್ಟನೆ (tattane) = Suddenly or Quickly (ಕೂಡಲೆ in Kannada).

4. 'ಗುಟ್ಟು' ಇದರ ಅರ್ಥ? / What does 'ಗುಟ್ಟು' mean?

💡 Something private you tell only to a best friend.
✅ ಗುಟ್ಟು (guttu) = Secret (ರಹಸ್ಯ in Kannada). Something told quietly to only one person.

5. ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಕುಳಿತುಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತದೆ? / Where does the butterfly sit (in the poem)?

💡 Butterflies love colourful plants with sweet smells.
✅ In the poem, the butterfly sits near a flower (ಹೂವಿನ ಹತ್ತಿರ). The poet asks it to share the flower's secret.

6. 'ಪೋರಿ' ಇದರ ಅರ್ಥ? / What does 'ಪೋರಿ' mean?

💡 A sweet word for a small girl.
✅ ಪೋರಿ (pori) = Little girl / naughty girl (ತುಂಟಿ in Kannada).

7. ಕವಿ ಚಿಟ್ಟೆಗೆ ಏನು ಕೊಡುತ್ತೇನೆಂದು ಹೇಳುತ್ತಾರೆ? / What does the poet say he will give to the butterfly?

💡 Two tasty foods — one is hot and the other is sweet.
✅ In the poem, the poet promises to give the butterfly hot dosa (ಬಿಸಿ ಬಿಸಿ ದೋಸೆ) and sweet honey (ಸಿಹಿ ಸಿಹಿ ಜೇನು) to come inside.
