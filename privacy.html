Privacy Policy — Class 3 Practice

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Class 3 Practice ("the app") is a free educational app for Class 3 students, built and maintained by Shabanam Bano Ansari. This page explains what information the app does — and does not — collect.

What the app does

The app shows practice quizzes and study material for school subjects (Maths, Science, English, and others). All quiz content is loaded from a public website (shabanam1982.github.io/class3_quiz) and displayed inside the app. Quiz pages are saved on your device after you first open them, so they keep working without an internet connection.

What information we collect

None. The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or enter any personal information. It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services of any kind. It does not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data, and does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any to begin with.

Internet access

The app connects to the internet only to download quiz pages and a list of available subjects from the website above. These are public files with no personal information in them. No information about you or your device is sent to that website or to us.

Information stored on your device

To let quizzes work offline, the app saves a local copy of quiz pages and your quiz answers and scores on your own device's storage. This information stays on your device — it is never uploaded anywhere, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Children's privacy

This app is designed for use by children studying the Class 3 curriculum, typically with a parent, teacher, or tutor's supervision. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, it does not gather, store, or share any data that would be subject to children's privacy laws such as COPPA.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date.
