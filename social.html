Chapter 4: The Air Around Us 💨

💡 Learn about air, wind, and how we use them.

Q1. Can we see air?

Q2. Which shows that air is moving?

Q3. Moving air is called ___?

Q4. We need air to ___?

Q5. Which one flies using air?

Q6. Fill in blank: A ___ works because of wind.

Hint: Has big blades that spin. Ends with "mill".

📖 Short Answer Questions

1. Write two uses of air.
1. Air helps us breathe.
2. Air helps kites, windmills, and boats to move.
2. How do we know air is around us?
We cannot see air, but we can feel it. When leaves shake or clothes fly, we know air is around us.

Chapter 5: Water We Drink 💧

💡 Learn about water sources and saving water.

Q1. Which is a source of water?

Q2. Which is NOT a source of water?

Q3. Clean drinking water should be ___?

Q4. Rain water is collected in ___?

Q5. Which is a good habit to save water?

Q6. Fill in blank: We should ___ water because it is precious.

Hint: 4 letters, means "to keep safe".

📖 Short Answer Questions

1. Name four sources of water.
1. River
2. Well
3. Pond / Lake
4. Rain
2. Write two uses of water.
1. We drink water to stay healthy.
2. We use water for cooking, bathing, washing, and watering plants.
3. How can we save water?
1. Close the tap after use.
2. Do not waste water while brushing or bathing.
3. Fix leaking taps.

Chapter 6: My Community 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

💡 A community is a group of people who live together and help each other.

Q1. A group of people living together is called a ___?

Q2. Who is a member of your family community?

Q3. Who helps you learn in school?

Q4. Who keeps our streets clean?

Q5. Which is good behavior in community?

Q6. Fill in blank: In our community we should ___ each other.

Hint: 4 letters, when someone does you a favour.

📖 Short Answer Questions

1. What is a community?
A community is a group of people who live together in one place and help each other. Family, neighbours, and friends are part of our community.
2. Name three helpers in your community.
1. Teacher (helps us learn)
2. Doctor (treats us when sick)
3. Postman (brings letters)
3. How can you be a good community member?
1. Help others when they need.
2. Keep surroundings clean.
3. Respect elders and be kind to everyone.

Chapter 7: Occupations 👩‍⚕️👨‍🌾

💡 Occupation means the work people do to earn money.

Q1. Who grows food for us?

Q2. Who treats sick people?

Q3. Who stitches clothes?

Q4. Who makes things from wood?

Q5. Match Occupations with Their Tools

Tap one from the left, then tap its matching tool.

Q6. Fill in blank: A person who cuts hair is a ___.

Hint: 6 letters, works in a salon.

📖 Short Answer Questions

1. What is occupation?
Occupation is the work that people do to earn money and help others. Farming, teaching, and doctoring are occupations.
2. Write any four occupations.
1. Farmer
2. Doctor
3. Teacher
4. Tailor
3. Why are farmers important to us?
Farmers grow food like rice, wheat, fruits, and vegetables for us. Without farmers, we cannot get food. So farmers are very important.
