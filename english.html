4. Fill in the Blanks

💡 Read the sentence carefully and type the correct word.

Q1. The sun rises in the ___.

Hint: 4 letters, direction.

Q2. A ___ says "meow".

Hint: 3 letters, a small animal.

Q3. We drink ___ when we are thirsty.

Hint: 5 letters, comes from taps.

Q4. There are ___ days in a week.

Hint: A number, spelled with letters.

Q5. We go to ___ to learn.

Hint: 6 letters, has teachers and children.

Q6. Birds have ___ to fly.

Hint: 5 letters, on both sides of the body.

Hint: 5 letters, a colour.

5. Reading & Comprehension

💡 Read the small story below carefully, then answer the questions.

Story: The Clever Crow 🐦

One hot summer day, a crow was very thirsty. He flew here and there looking for water. At last, he saw a pot of water. But the water was very little at the bottom. The crow was clever. He picked up small stones and dropped them into the pot one by one. Soon, the water came up to the top. The crow drank the water and flew away happily.

Q1. Who was thirsty in the story?

Q2. What season was it?

Q3. What did the crow put in the pot?

Q4. Why did the water rise?

Q5. What kind of bird was the crow?

📖 Moral Question

What is the moral (lesson) of this story?
The moral is:
"Where there is a will, there is a way."
Or: "Think cleverly, and you can solve any problem."
